# BA-010 — MES opening-range continuation

Version 1, declared September 9, 2026 America/Chicago, before computing strategy
returns on any MES history. An expired MESZ5 session from November 3, 2025 was
previously downloaded only to verify API access and bar integrity. It was not
used to choose these rules. This is a chronological validation protocol, not a
claim that the S&P market's historical regimes are unknown to the researchers.

## Hypothesis and scope

A breakout from the first 30 minutes of the U.S. cash session sometimes reflects
persistent directional order flow. A single intraday continuation trade may
capture that movement after fees. This is an unproven hypothesis; this experiment
does not claim an academic or empirical edge in advance.

Trade one CME Micro E-mini S&P 500 future (MES) at a time, long or short, with no
overnight position. This is a new strategy, unrelated to the missing Coinbase
contracts in BA-009B. No account connection or real-money order is authorized.

## Fixed strategy

All times use America/New_York, including daylight saving changes.

1. Only full NYSE sessions scheduled 09:30–16:00 are eligible. Scheduled holidays
   and early closes are excluded in advance using a versioned historical calendar.
2. Opening range: high/low of minute bars labeled **09:30 through 09:59**.
3. Inspect bars labeled **10:00 through 11:28**. The first completed close at least
   one tick above the opening high or below the opening low is the day's sole
   candidate. An ineligible first breakout consumes the day; no later replacement.
4. Place the simulated market entry at the open of the bar **two minutes after
   the signal bar label**. This leaves a full minute after the signal bar closes.
   Latest entry is 11:30. Bar publication/market execution remain approximations.
5. Initial stop: midpoint of the opening range, rounded down to a valid tick for
   a long and up for a short. It never trails. No profit target.
6. Eligibility uses the signal close, one adverse tick at entry and exit, and
   round-trip fees. The resulting planned loss at the stop must be **$25–$75**.
   This is a research risk budget, not an optimized threshold or guaranteed loss.
7. Do not cancel or erase the order based on the later opening price. Gaps and
   resulting risk overruns remain in the result. An entry already through its
   fixed stop is immediately flattened at the opening price with adverse slippage.
8. If not stopped earlier, exit at the **15:55 open**. At most one entry per day.

## Execution and accounting

- MES multiplier $5/index point, tick 0.25 points = $1.25. Compute in integer ticks
  and cents to avoid unit/rounding ambiguity.
- Base per-side charges: $0.25 broker + $0.35 exchange + $0.01 regulatory = $0.61.
  These are current public assumptions, applied consistently across history.
- Base slippage: one adverse tick per fill. Ordinary round-trip friction is
  $1.22 fees + $2.50 slippage = **$3.72**.
- Stress: two adverse ticks per fill, preserving base signal eligibility and
  candidate orders. Capital-constrained stress may stop earlier; also show a
  fixed-one-contract diagnostic to distinguish that effect from execution costs.
- A long stop crossed by an opening gap fills at min(open, stop) minus slippage;
  otherwise a low touching the stop fills at stop minus slippage. Reverse for
  shorts. Check the entry bar. Time/risk exits at an opening price take precedence
  over that minute's subsequent high/low.
- No same-bar target/stop ordering problem exists because there is no target.
  OHLC still cannot establish exact executable quotes or liquidity through a jump.
- Charge **$1.55/month** for CME L1, on the first eligible session each month,
  including months after a simulated halt. Hosting is assumed to use the user's
  existing computer; extra hosting/electricity and taxes are not modeled. Research
  acquisition credits/cost are reported separately. Idle cash earns zero in the
  simulated account; 4%/6% cash accounts are separate comparators.

## Capital and stopping rules

Each independent test starts at $5,000, with one contract and no resizing or
deposits. Require $4,240 before entry: a rounded current $3,240 initial-margin
proxy plus $1,000 reserve. This is deliberately conservative for intraday entry,
but **does not reconstruct historical or account-specific broker margin**.
Falling below this entry floor permanently idles the test and is a feasibility
failure. It can bind after about $760 of initial losses, before the loss stop.

Monitor liquidation-value equity at completed minute closes, including remaining
exit friction. At **$1,000 below the highest observed minute-close/flat equity**,
schedule an exit at the next available minute open and halt permanently. A stop
already triggered takes precedence. A realized exit reaching the same threshold
also halts. Gaps/slippage can exceed the threshold. Report observed drawdown and
a conservative intrabar high/low bound separately; do not infer tick ordering
from OHLC. Unconstrained one-contract diagnostics cannot rescue a stopped pilot.

## Data and audit

Databento GLBX.MDP3, MES.v.0, 2021-01-01 through 2026-01-01 exclusive, minute bars.
Save immutable inputs and date-dependent mappings to actual contracts. Volume
ranking uses the vendor's previous-day volume rule; never choose a contract using
that day's eventual volume. No P&L crosses a roll because positions close daily.
Verify one mapped raw MES contract throughout each observed session/trade.

Validate price ticks/OHLC, ordering, duplicate timestamps, instrument IDs, session
membership and missing intervals. No execution-price forward fill. Missing data
known before entry can cause an abstention. Missing data after entry makes the
trade unresolved and blocks a verdict; it must never erase an unfavorable trade.
Future completeness must not decide whether a past trade was taken.

## Evaluation order and frozen gates

1. Development: **2021–2023**, one continuous $5,000 account from January 2021.
   Run base and stress plus unrestricted one-contract diagnostics. If base net
   P&L is not positive, the pilot halts, or held-position data are unresolved,
   do not evaluate 2024/2025. Retire/park this version with its actual failure.
2. Only if development passes, freeze tested code and calendar hashes, evaluate
   2024, then 2025 **without changes**. The primary out-of-sample account starts
   at $5,000 in January 2024 and carries its equity/high water through 2025.
   Do not restart a failed 2024 account to manufacture a successful 2025 pilot.
3. Hard promotion gates: reconciled data; positive net P&L in each of 2024 and
   2025; combined 2024–2025 CAGR >6%; no capital-floor or drawdown halt; positive
   stressed OOS P&L. Current margin assumptions and execution require later checks.
4. Statistical gate: stationary block bootstrap of daily net **dollar** P&L,
   including no-trade days and operating fees, 10,000 draws, seed 1010, mean block
   length 10 eligible sessions; show prespecified 5/20-session sensitivity.
   Do not compound sampled percentage returns as if contract size scales.
   Report 95% intervals for mean daily P&L and annualized arithmetic dollars.
   Require the OOS interval for mean excess over the corresponding 6% cash
   account's total gain per eligible session to exclude zero before claiming
   evidence of beating cash. Otherwise positive point results are inconclusive.
5. Report trade counts, abstentions, annual P&L, gross/friction, concentration in
   the five largest winners, gap risk overruns and all halt dates. No arbitrary
   number of trades alone proves adequate statistical power.

A passed historical screen can justify a quote/fill audit and prospective paper
test, never a live-trading authorization. No parameter grid, sign reversal, or
new variant is fitted to these results. Any later revision receives a new version
and discloses the historical periods already used.
