# BA-001 — Multi-Asset Trend

Status: **locked for initial implementation**
Locked: **2026-09-04**, before running BoringAlpha on historical market data

## Hypothesis

Medium-term total-return trends may persist long enough to trade. Assigning a
risky asset's fixed sleeve to cash when its trailing return does not exceed the
cash return may reduce severe drawdowns without destroying the portfolio's
long-run risk-adjusted return.

## Universe

| Symbol | Sleeve | Target weight when active |
|---|---|---:|
| SPY | US large-cap equity | 12.5% |
| IWM | US small-cap equity | 12.5% |
| EFA | Developed international equity | 12.5% |
| EEM | Emerging-market equity | 12.5% |
| IEF | Intermediate US Treasury | 12.5% |
| TLT | Long-duration US Treasury | 12.5% |
| GLD | Gold | 12.5% |
| DBC | Broad commodities | 12.5% |

The universe is fixed before the test. An unavailable sleeve remains cash; it
is not redistributed to active assets.

## Signal

At the close of the final trading session of each calendar month:

```text
asset_return = TR_close[t] / TR_close[t - 12 calendar months] - 1
cash_return  = cash_index[t] / cash_index[t - 12 calendar months] - 1

active = asset_return > cash_return
```

The anchor is the latest shared trading session on or before the calendar date
12 months earlier. All eight assets must have the required history before the
portfolio begins.

## Portfolio and execution

- Active sleeve: 12.5% target weight.
- Inactive sleeve: 0%; its capital remains cash.
- Signal timestamp: month-end close.
- Execution timestamp: next available shared session open.
- Direction: long or cash only.
- Leverage: none.
- Rebalancing: monthly, including active sleeves returning to their fixed
  target weights.
- Base modeled cost: 10 basis points per traded notional, per side.
- Cash earns the supplied one-session cash factor.

## Benchmarks

1. Cash.
2. Static equal-weight allocation to the same eight sleeves, rebalanced on the
   same schedule with the same cost model.
3. A global equity buy-and-hold benchmark when real market data is introduced.

## Evaluation protocol

- Development: 2007–2017.
- Validation: 2018–2021.
- Sealed test: 2022 through the latest complete dataset.
- Inspect performance across the global financial crisis, the pandemic shock,
  the subsequent recovery, and the equity/bond drawdown of the inflationary
  period.
- Evaluate the pre-registered 12-month rule first. The 9- and 15-month rules
  are stability checks, not candidates from which to select the best result.

## Advancement criteria

Advance only if results are economically coherent after costs, broadly stable
across periods and nearby lookbacks, not dominated by one sleeve, reproducible,
and useful relative to both cash and the static benchmark. Performance must
also remain meaningful at twice the base cost assumption.

Classify the outcome as **advance**, **inconclusive**, or **reject**. A rejected
strategy still counts as a successful test of the laboratory.

## Questions reserved for later variants

These are valid research questions, but none may change BA-001 after its results
are known:

- Does a 9- or 15-month lookback show a stable neighborhood around 12 months?
- Does volatility scaling improve risk without making turnover or parameter
  sensitivity worse?
- Does a short-term pullback entry improve implementation versus immediate
  next-session rebalancing?
- Should correlation clusters cap economically duplicated exposures?
- Does any public-information or sentiment feature add value after a price-only
  baseline and realistic timestamp cutoffs?
