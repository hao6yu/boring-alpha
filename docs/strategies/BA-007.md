# BA-007 — Crypto cross-sectional selection: does ranking separate, net of every cost, with the market taken out

Status: **pre-registered 2026-09-08, before any signal was computed on any real price, rank, or spread in this family.** The data spine
that feeds this charter is committed (`tools/fetch_perps.py`, decision record in
[`docs/decisions/2026-09-08-ba007-crypto-data-source.md`](../decisions/2026-09-08-ba007-crypto-data-source.md)); its full-universe fetch may
still be running as this document is locked, which is the point — the rules below are the rules because they answer the question, not
because any table rewarded them. Parent family: none in this repository; BA-001..006 timed or sized *one* asset, and every one of them
ended Inconclusive, no-go, Redundant, or failed on its own gates. This charter asks a structurally different question: not whether an
asset will rise, but whether a **ranking across ~30 assets** separates future winners from losers by more than the cost of acting on the
separation, with the market's direction cancelled by construction.

## 1. The question

**Does a pre-registered cross-sectional ranking of Binance USDT-M perpetuals, held long-top/short-bottom in a dollar-neutral book at a
weekly cadence, earn more than it costs — in the development window AND the validation window — and does it beat its own controls?**

The comparator is not an index. For a market-neutral book the Dominance Rule comparator is **zero after costs**: doing nothing. A
long-short spread that does not clear its own fees and funding is Redundant, whatever its Sharpe looks like before costs.

Why this question can be answered when the one-asset questions could not: a monthly timing rule on one ETF produces ~15 effective
independent observations per decade, whose honest verdict is the word Inconclusive (BA-001's). A weekly cross-section across 30 names
produces ~30 near-independent ranking bets per rebalance and ~52 rebalances a year — thousands of observations in the development window
alone. The instrument finally gets a sample its statistics can resolve.

## 2. Prior evidence and its discount

| Claim | Source | Discount applied |
|---|---|---|
| Cross-sectional momentum is a documented anomaly in many asset classes | Jegadeesh & Titman (1993) equities; Asness, Moskowitz & Pedersen (2013) "Value and Momentum Everywhere", which includes global futures and currencies | Academic, survivorship-prone, gross of realistic costs at retail scale; used as motivation, never as evidence |
| Crypto exhibits cross-sectional momentum at short horizons | Public practitioner literature (e.g. Liu & Tsyvinski, "Risks and Returns of Cryptocurrency", Review of Financial Studies 2021; long-short crypto momentum studies) | Author cautions: crypto momentum evidence is mostly pre-2022, pre-this-archive, and gross of the fee tier this account would actually pay |
| Perp funding is a harvestable carry premium | Structural: longs pay shorts when funding is positive; the rate is bounded by exchange clamps and mean-reverts after squeezes | The premium is compensation for holding the crowded side during squeezes; the tail is the product being sold, and this charter prices it, not ignores it |

The discount in full: none of these establish that the edge survives **this** fee tier, **this** weekly cadence, **this** universe's
dispersion, or **this** archive's delistings. That is precisely what the gates below exist to price.

## 3. Instrument and universe (locked)

- **Instrument:** Binance USDT-margined perpetual futures, daily bars and funding events, from the repository's own spine
  (`data/perps/current`, `perps_daily.csv`, `funding_events.csv`). Prices are the daily UTC close (`close` column); the in-progress
  session is never an observation.
- **Eligibility at each rebalance date t** (point-in-time, computed only from data before t):
  1. the symbol has an observed close on t;
  2. the symbol has **≥ 60 calendar days** of observed closes before t (every signal and volume window fits inside its own life);
  3. rank eligible symbols by **trailing 30-day median daily quote volume** (the `quote_volume` column), descending.
- **The universe at t is the top 30** eligible symbols. If fewer than 30 are eligible, the book runs on what is eligible and reports the
  count; if fewer than **12** are eligible, the week is skipped and reported. The numbers 30 / 60 / 12 were fixed for coverage and
  statistical width before any ranking was computed and were not scanned.
- Because the spine archives delisted symbols, a symbol that was in the top 30 and later died stays eligible until its death — the
  universe inherits survivorship bias from **nowhere**. This is the property that makes the cross-section's history real.

## 4. Signals (locked; exactly two, and no others may be scored on seen data without a new charter)

Each signal is one number per eligible symbol per rebalance, ranked; the book holds **long the top tercile, short the bottom tercile**,
equal-weight within each side (at N=30: 10 names per side; at N=18, 6 per side; terciles of the eligible count, rounded down).

1. **XS-MOM — cross-sectional momentum.** The symbol's total close-to-close return over the **7 calendar days** ending at t. Rank
   descending: highest past-week return ranks first (long side).
2. **XS-CARRY — funding carry.** The symbol's **summed realized funding rate over the trailing 72 hours** ending at t (sum of event
   `rate` values whose UTC timestamp falls in (t−72h, t], regardless of the event's interval; a 1h cadence symbol is summed the same
   way). Rank **ascending**: the most negative (shorts paying) ranks first (long side). Holding the long leg *receives* when funding is
   negative; the short leg *receives* when funding is positive — the book is positioned to be paid for holding the uncrowded side.

**Multiple-testing accounting, declared in advance:** this family scores exactly two signals on the same construction. Each must clear
every gate on its own. No combination, no lookback neighbour, no tercile/quintile variation, and no third signal may be evaluated on
development or validation data under this charter; wanting one is a new charter with its own pre-registered gates.

## 5. Portfolio construction and execution (locked)

- **Cadence:** rebalance every **7 days**, on the first session date of each ISO week present in the archive (Monday if served, else the
  first served day of that week). One decision a week, made on data that closed before the position exists.
- **Weights:** dollar-neutral, **gross 1.0**: long-side notional +1.0/2 per side split equally across the long tercile, short-side
  notional −1.0/2 split equally across the short tercile.
- **Beta:** portfolio beta versus BTCUSDT is **measured and reported every period as a diagnostic**, with no gate on it — dollar-
  neutrality plus tercile breadth is the construction; a beta overlay would add a moving part this charter did not pre-register.
- **Execution:** at the daily close of the rebalance date (the only price the spine has). The trade is charged the taker fee on the full
  traded notional of every position change, both sides. No intraweek trading: a delisting mid-week closes the position at that day's
  close (or the last close before death) and the cash sits until the next rebalance.
- **Funding accrual:** every position pays or receives each realized funding event on its notional, for every event between entry and
  exit. Long pays positive funding; short receives it. This is charged daily from the raw events, never approximated by an average.

## 6. Costs (locked)

- **Primary fee scenario:** **5.0 bps taker per side** on traded notional (Binance USDT-M VIP0 posted taker, 0.05%, declared; the
  operator's actual tier belongs in the venue-fee record when one exists, exactly as the Coinbase family requires).
- **Stress scenario:** **10.0 bps per side** (2×). A verdict that does not survive the stress scenario is not a pass.
- Funding is not a scenario: it is charged at the realized rates, both directions, always.
- Slippage is **not modelled** — daily bars cannot measure impact, and pretending otherwise would be the flattering kind of precision.
  The declared remedy is the paper book (Phase 4), where a fill is a fill. This limitation is load-bearing: the gates below must be
  cleared by margins wide enough that a plausible impact at this liquidity (top-30 by volume) does not flip them, and the paper book is
  the test of that claim.

## 7. Evaluation periods (locked)

| period | window | role |
|---|---|---|
| development | **2020-02-01 → 2022-12-31** | the mania of 2021 and the collapse of 2022, both inside one window; where the signal must first earn its verdict |
| validation | **2023-01-01 → 2024-12-31** | a different regime; the BA-001 lesson is that a development advantage that reverses in validation is the finding |
| sealed | **2025-01-01 → latest complete session** | never opened by this charter; requested only after development and validation both pass, by a separate deliberate reveal |

Data before 2020-02-01 is warm-up only (60-day history floor + 30-day volume window + 7-day signal window). The engine truncates at each
period's boundary before anything is computed, as the equity family's seals do. **This family consumes no BA-TREND observation**: its
archive, its venue, and its sealed window are its own; the equity family's sealed window remains unspent and untouched by this charter.

## 8. Gates and verdict (locked; computed, never typed in)

All gates are evaluated per signal, per period, on the net-of-everything daily spread return series (long minus short, fees and funding
charged).

- **G1 — the spread earns, honestly.** Annualized daily-mean net spread return > 0, AND the stationary block-bootstrap 95% interval of
  the annualized mean (block length 10 trading days, 2,000 resamples, seeded) **excludes zero**.
- **G2 — both windows, no exceptions.** G1 must hold in development AND in validation. A signal that earns in one window and not the
  other is classified **Inconclusive**, and Inconclusive here kills the candidate (there is no third window to shop for).
- **G3 — the controls must lose.** (a) The **reversed** book (long the bottom tercile, short the top) must not earn more than the signal
  in the same period, net. (b) The signal's development annualized mean must exceed the **median of 20 scrambled-rank books** (random
  permutation of the weekly ranks, seeds 0..19, fixed before any run).
- **G4 — the stress survives.** Every G1/G2/G3 result must also hold at 10 bps per side.
- **Verdict mechanics:** a `classify` step computes PASS / FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE from the artifacts; the verdict is arithmetic on the
  records, never a sentence anyone writes. PASS = eligible to request the sealed reveal and (separately) paper-book integration. FAIL or
  INCONCLUSIVE = the charter's question is answered **no** for this construction, and the honest-stop note is the deliverable.

## 9. Negative controls and what each accuses

- **Reversed ranks** — accuses the signal of being a sign error or a cost artifact: if reversing wins, the "edge" is a short on its own
  hypothesis.
- **Scrambled ranks (20 seeds)** — accuses the construction of earning the tercile structure's incidental exposure rather than the
  ranking's information.
- **Gross-minus-net** — accuses costs: the gross spread is reported beside the net one, and the gap is the price of existing.
- **Long-side-only and short-side-only legs** — reported as diagnostics: a "spread" that is really one leveraged leg wearing a hedge is
  the classic cross-sectional self-deception, and this decomposition is how it gets caught.

## 10. Limitations, stated before the first run

1. **Daily closes are not fills.** The book is priced at the close it decided on, which no one could have traded in size. The paper book
   exists because of this sentence.
2. **Volume is quote-volume in USDT terms**; the USDT/USD peg is a real risk the book's accounting cannot diversify away.
3. **Survivorship is handled at the universe level** (delisted symbols are archived and eligible at their point-in-time ranks), but the
   archive's own completeness is bounded by its manifest: BTCUSDT alone shows 23 day-holes inside served months, reported, not stitched.
   A hole inside a signal window removes that symbol from eligibility that week (the eligibility rules require observed closes; silence
   is not a price).
4. **Two signals, one construction, one fee grid is the whole family.** The surest way this charter could lie is by multiplying variants
   until one clears — so the count of scored variants is itself part of the pre-registration.
5. **The carry signal's tail is the product it sells.** Positive carry harvests are exposed to squeeze episodes exactly when dispersion
   explodes; the gates price the mean, and the reported drawdown is where the tail shows up. Neither the gates nor the charter pretend
   the tail away.

## 11. Stopping rule

- If development fails G1 or G3, or fails G4 at either fee: **stop; write the honest-stop note.** The cross-sectional question is
  answered for this construction; no tercile is widened, no lookback is nudged.
- If development passes and validation fails: **stop; write the honest-stop note,** with the reversal itself documented — that is the
  BA-001 lesson wearing this charter's clothes.
- If both pass and the controls lose: request the sealed reveal under the family journal discipline, and only then Phase 4.
