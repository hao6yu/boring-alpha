# BA-008 — Funding momentum: the mirror image BA-007 was not allowed to score

Status: **pre-registered 2026-09-09, before a single graded session of this hypothesis existed anywhere on disk.** Parent:
[BA-007](BA-007.md), whose sweep measured, as an un-promoted observation, that the *reversed* carry book — long the highest-funding names,
short the lowest — earned **+38.28%/yr in development and +45.23%/yr in validation** while the carry book itself failed 0/6 gates. This
charter promotes that observation to a hypothesis and gives it the only clean test a post-hoc observation can have: **every graded
session lies after this document's lock.** No backtest over 2020–2025 may be run for this signal, not even once, because every candle in
that span has already been seen by the process that generated the hypothesis — re-running it would be tuning wearing a lab coat.

## 1. The question

**Does the cross-section of perp funding rates predict relative returns positively — long the highest-funding third, short the lowest —
by enough to clear BA-007's gates on data that arrived after this charter was locked?**

The mechanism this charter commits to testing: on this archive, funding intensity behaved as an **attention and flow proxy** — the most
crowded longs were the mania's winners, not its victims. If that asymmetry persists forward, the ranking has information; if it was a
2020–2024 regime artifact, the forward test says so.

The honest prior: post-hoc observations shrink when tested forward. The seen-data estimate is what the hypothesis promises; the forward
verdict measures what it delivers. Nothing in this document may quote the seen-data numbers as an expectation — they appear here only as
the record of where the hypothesis came from.

## 3. Instrument, universe, construction — identical to BA-007 by reference

Universe (§3 of BA-007): top **30** by trailing **30-day** median daily quote volume, **60-day** history floor, point-in-time, delisted
symbols eligible at their own ranks, weeks under **12** scored symbols skipped. Construction (§5): weekly rebalance on the first served
session of each ISO week, dollar-neutral tercile book at **gross 1.0**, equal weight within side, execution at the daily close, delistings
closed at their last observed price, funding charged at realized rates from the day after entry. Costs (§6): **5 bps taker per side
primary, 10 bps stress**, funding always realized. Diagnostics (§5, §9): beta versus BTCUSDT, annualized one-way turnover, gross-minus-net
decomposition, long-only and short-only legs — reported every period, gating nothing.

## 4. The signal (locked)

**XS-FMOM** — rank eligible symbols by trailing **72-hour summed realized funding** (the same sum BA-007's CARRY used), **descending**:
the **highest** funding ranks first, the long side holds the top tercile, the short side holds the bottom tercile.

This is, deliberately and confessedly, **BA-007's XS-CARRY with the sign flipped.** That is not a defect; it *is* the observation. Exactly
one signal is registered. No lookback variation, no tercile variation, no funding-window variation, no combined signal — the family pays
for its post-hoc origin by scoring this one construction and nothing else, and any variant is a new charter that this document's failure
record will follow like a debt.

## 5. Evaluation protocol: forward-only, graded on data that did not exist at lock

- **Graded window:** every session on or after **2026-10-01**. The first graded week's return period lies wholly after the lock (lock
  2026-09-09; first graded rebalance the first served Monday of October). Sessions before 2026-10-01 may warm up the machinery (history
  floor, volume window) but no graded return may touch them, and **any FMOM verdict computed on pre-October sessions is void by
  definition** — the paper book's ledger begins at its creation, which is the enforcement.
- **Evidence bars, locked now:** the verdict is graded twice — at **52 graded weeks** (the minimum) and again at **104** (the target).
  Below 52 weeks there is no verdict, only accumulation; the charter refuses to pronounce on less.
- **Grading rule:** at each evidence bar, compute the gates on all graded sessions to that point. Between bars, the book simply seals
  monthly and says nothing.

## 6. Gates (locked; BA-007's, unchanged in kind)

Computed from artifacts by the engine, never typed in:

- **G1 — the forward spread earns.** Annualized daily-mean net spread return > 0 AND the stationary block-bootstrap 95% interval (block
  10, 2,000 resamples, seeded) excludes zero, over all graded sessions to the evidence bar.
- **G3 — the controls must lose.** (a) The reversed book — long lowest funding, short highest, which is exactly BA-007's failed CARRY —
  must not out-earn the candidate. (b) The candidate must beat the median of **20 seeded scrambled-rank books** (seeds 0–19).
- **G4 — the stress survives.** G1 must also hold at 10 bps per side.
- **Verdict at 52 weeks:** PASS (all gates) → continue accumulating to 104 and report eligibility, not a funding decision — money is out
  of this document's scope. FAIL (G1's interval excludes zero on the *downside*, or reversed/scrambled win) → **stop; the hypothesis is
  dead and the honest-stop note is the deliverable.** INCONCLUSIVE (interval contains zero) → continue to 104 weeks, once; a second
  INCONCLUSIVE at 104 is terminal.
- **No early looks:** no verdict may be printed, computed, or hinted at between evidence bars. The monthly seal records the book's state;
  it does not grade it.

## 7. Negative controls (unchanged in kind, unchanged in accusation)

Reversed ranks (the failed CARRY, which must keep losing), 20 scrambled seeds (the construction must not earn on noise), gross-minus-net
(the price of existing), long-only and short-only legs (whether the "spread" is one leveraged leg wearing a hedge). The reversed control
carries the sharpest accusation in this charter: it is the exact book that failed 0/6 gates on seen data, so if it wins forward, the
seen-data reversal was itself the fluke.

## 8. Limitations, stated before the first graded session

1. **The hypothesis was born from seen data.** The forward protocol is the only honest test, and the honest prior is shrinkage: the
   seen-data +38–45%/yr is the ceiling of what the forward test could confirm, not an expectation of it.
2. **Regime risk is the named risk.** The observation spans a mania and a recovery; 2026+ may rhyme with neither. The charter does not
   claim the effect is structural — it claims the test is clean.
3. **Power is modest at 52 weeks.** Roughly 52 weekly rebalances × ~20 positions is thousands of near-independent bets, but the bootstrap
   on a fat-tailed spread needs its interval respected: INCONCLUSIVE is a real outcome and is treated as terminal at 104 weeks.
4. **Daily closes are not fills** (BA-007 §6, unchanged), and the USDT peg is a real risk the accounting cannot diversify away.
5. **This charter consumes no BA-TREND observation and touches no sealed window.** Its test data is the future; the past is warm-up only.

## 9. Stopping rule

- 52 weeks: PASS → continue to 104. Downside-significant FAIL → stop, honest-stop note. INCONCLUSIVE → continue to 104.
- 104 weeks: PASS → the hypothesis survives; funding a live account is a separate decision outside this document. FAIL or INCONCLUSIVE →
  stop, honest-stop note, the funding-momentum question is answered for this construction.
- At any point: three consecutive months of failed seals or a dead spine pauses the calendar, it does not amend the gates.
