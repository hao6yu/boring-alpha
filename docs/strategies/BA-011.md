# BA-011 — MES late-day sign momentum

Version 1, September 9, 2026. Registered before calculating this candidate's
historical returns. The user explicitly authorized one fixed test after the
paper-only feasibility screen could not determine single-S&P trade expectancy.
That uncertainty is the purpose of this experiment. BA-010's 2021–2023 results
are already seen; 2024–2025 have not been used for strategy evaluation.

## Hypothesis and exact rule

Late-day hedging demand may reinforce the day's market direction. The published
rest-of-day sign predictor motivates this test; its portfolio returns do not
establish MES returns. [Baltussen et al., Eq.12 and Table A1](https://academicweb.nd.edu/~zda/intramom.pdf).
This is a delayed, fixed-one-contract implementation with account controls,
not an exact replication of a diversified academic portfolio.

All times are America/New_York with historical daylight saving changes.

1. Only scheduled full NYSE sessions, 09:30–16:00, are eligible. Use the immediately
   preceding actual scheduled session, including early closes in the calendar.
   If that predecessor is not a full session, abstain. Do not search backward
   for an earlier full session.
2. Reference price: the preceding session's bar labeled **15:59 close**. Its
   actual contract symbol and instrument ID must match today's contract.
   Missing predecessor/reference or a contract change causes causal abstention.
   Never subtract unadjusted prices across a futures roll.
3. Signal: today's **15:29 close** minus that reference. Go long one MES contract
   if positive; otherwise short one contract, including exact equality. There
   is no magnitude filter, volatility scaling, or alternative signal.
4. Require the signal bar and the pre-entry bar labeled **15:30**. If either is
   missing before entry, abstain. The simulated order is committed for the
   **15:31 open**, leaving a full minute after the signal bar ends. A future
   entry gap cannot erase, resize, or reverse the submitted order.
5. Exit at the **16:00 open**, unless the account drawdown rule exits earlier.
   One entry per eligible day; never hold overnight. There is no per-trade
   technical stop, target or trailing price stop. BA-010's opening-range stop
   and $25–$75 signal eligibility do not belong to this hypothesis.

Missing committed entry or any held minute through the required exit makes the
position unresolved and refuses the entire verdict. Missing future data after
an already resolved exit cannot erase the trade. No price forward fill, no
future-completeness-based removal of sessions, and no realized-return filters.

## Execution, costs and capital

- MES: $5 per index point; 0.25-point tick worth $1.25. Integer ticks/cents.
- Fees: $0.61 per side. Base slippage one adverse tick per fill; stress two.
  Round-trip friction is $3.72 base and $6.22 stress. These are previously
  verified current public assumptions applied across history, not actual fills.
- Independent continuous $5,000 account for each of four models: base pilot,
  stress pilot, base unrestricted and stress unrestricted. One contract; no
  deposits, scaling or annual resets. Unrestricted models are diagnostic only.
- Charge $1.55 on the first eligible session of every month, including months
  after a halt. Idle cash earns zero. Research acquisition is reported separately;
  no additional purchase is needed. Extra hosting/electricity and taxes excluded.
- Pilot entry/flat equity floor: **$4,240**. Falling below it permanently halts
  the pilot. This is a before-entry/realized-equity constraint, not an additional
  intratrade exit trigger. It uses a conservative current margin proxy, not
  reconstructed historical/account-specific margin.
- Observe liquidation equity at each completed held minute close, including
  the remaining exit friction, and at flat events. A **$1,000 drawdown** from
  the highest such equity triggers an exit at the next minute open and a
  permanent halt. A realized exit crossing the same limit also halts. The
  16:00 opening exit precedes any later high/low/close within its bar.
- Report overshoots and a separate conservative intrabar high/low drawdown
  envelope. The envelope is not an ordered tick path or a guaranteed loss cap.
  Stress may halt earlier; unrestricted diagnostics isolate cost differences.

## Gates and stopping the research

1. Development, 2021–2023: require resolved data, positive base-pilot net P&L
   and no base-pilot halt. Report base/stress and unrestricted diagnostics,
   annual net/gross/friction, cash comparisons, abstentions, worst trade,
   concentration and account drawdowns. A failure ends this candidate and this
   reopened research round; do not inspect its 2024–2025 strategy results.
2. A development pass allows the unchanged 2024–2025 test, one continuous $5,000
   account starting January 2024. Hard gates: reconciled/resolved data, positive
   net in each year, combined CAGR above 6%, no base-pilot halt, positive stress net.
3. Statistical gate: stationary circular block bootstrap of daily net **dollar**
   P&L including idle and monthly-fee days; 10,000 draws, seed 1010, mean block
   length 10 sessions, sensitivities 5 and 20. The 95% interval's lower bound for
   mean excess over the equivalent 6% cash gain per eligible session must be
   above zero. No compounding sampled returns as if contract size scaled.
4. Hard failure and statistical inconclusiveness are separate outcomes. A pass
   permits only later quote/fill audit and prospective paper validation. It
   does not authorize live trading or an increase in capital.

No parameter sweep, reverse-sign rescue, second timing signal, post-result stop
changes, or automatic next candidate. A code defect may be corrected only with
an explicit record; preserve invalidated results and disclose data already seen.
