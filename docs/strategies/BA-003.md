# BA-003 — Relative-Strength Tilt

Date: 2026-09-05. **Draft strategy plan — proposed, not approved or locked.**
No BA-003 implementation or historical evaluation has been performed as part
of this draft. This document does not authorize a run, a holdout reveal, or
trading. BA-001 and BA-002 retain their existing records.

## 1. The question

**With the same target investment budget as our passive benchmark, can a small,
slow-moving tilt toward stronger equity markets improve returns after costs
and modeled taxes, without worsening drawdown?**

BA-001/002 varied exposure according to each asset's trend against cash.
BA-003 instead varies allocation **inside the equity bucket**. It does not
move more money into equities when momentum looks good, or exit to cash when
everything falls. It remains related momentum research, not a new independent
research family.

The [BA-002 two-source result](../reviews/BA-002-source-sensitivity.md) motivates
changing the question: its seen-B shortfall existed before taxes as well as
after them. That report did not establish a causal decomposition of the loss;
it does not establish that this new signal will work.

Relative momentum has a research basis, but the published portfolios are not
this four-ETF, long-only, taxable strategy. The
[French momentum methodology](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/Data_Library/det_mom_factor.html)
uses prior months 2–12;
[Asness, Moskowitz and Pedersen (2013)](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Journal-Article/Value-and-Momentum-Everywhere)
study momentum across multiple markets. These motivate a hypothesis, not an
expected return or evidence that BA-003 has an edge.

## 2. Proposed portfolio and signal

Retain the eight ETFs and the benchmark's **60% invested / 40% cash** target.
This is not a conventional 60% stocks / 40% bonds portfolio.

| Component | Annual passive benchmark | BA-003 at establishment / annual reset |
|---|---:|---:|
| SPY, IWM, EFA, EEM | 7.5% each; 30% total | Top two: 10% each; bottom two: 5% each; 30% total |
| IEF, TLT | 7.5% each; 15% total | Unchanged |
| GLD | 7.5% | Unchanged |
| DBC | 7.5% | Unchanged |
| Cash | 40% before trading costs | Unchanged before trading costs |

Only SPY, IWM, EFA and EEM are ranked. Do not compare equity momentum directly
with Treasury duration, gold or commodity returns. Keep every ETF held; no
leverage, shorting, volatility sizing, cash signal or discretionary override.

At a scheduled decision close in calendar month **M**, score each equity ETF:

`score = TR_close(last session of M−1) / TR_close(last session of M−12) − 1`.

This is an eleven-month total return that skips the most recent month, often
called **12–1 momentum**. For the December 2020 decision, use the last session
of November 2020 divided by the last session of December 2019. Rank descending;
exact ties use the fixed order `SPY, IWM, EFA, EEM`. No fitted tie tolerance.
Negative scores still receive ranks: even if all four fall, select two leaders.
Missing/non-finite inputs halt the evaluation rather than change the universe.

The desired **within-equity** weights are `1/3, 1/3, 1/6, 1/6`, assigned by
rank. At a 30% equity allocation these become 10%, 10%, 5%, 5% of the account.
The tilt transfers 5% of account value relative to the equal-weight baseline;
it is not a concentrated winner-take-all strategy.

## 3. Trading schedule and no-trade band

- **Establishment:** invest on the first evaluation session using the preceding
  month-end decision, even if this is not a January/April/July/October entry.
- **January:** at the first shared session's open, reset the entire portfolio
  to the table above using the preceding December decision. This reset is
  unconditional; do not also execute a second quarterly rebalance that day.
- **April, July, October:** use the preceding March, June, September month-end
  close to review the equity bucket only. For each equity ETF, divide its
  current marked value by the total marked value of the four equity ETFs.
  Trade only if the maximum absolute difference from its desired within-equity
  weight is **at least 0.05** (five percentage points of the equity bucket,
  approximately 1.5 percentage points of the account at a 30% equity weight).
- If the band is breached, reset **all four** equity positions to their desired
  proportions. Otherwise hold all positions. All other months are holds.

The ranking and band decision use only that decision close and earlier data.
Execution is at the next shared session's open. On equity-only rebalances,
size against the equity bucket's value at that open, not stale closing dollar
amounts. Do not recheck the ranking or band after an overnight gap. Preserve
the bucket's total gross value; do not buy or sell IEF, TLT, GLD or DBC.
Gross equity purchases equal gross equity sales; transaction costs come out
of the cash reserve. Annual targets likewise use pre-trade account value and
costs reduce cash. Before execution, require `cash + gross sells − gross buys`
to cover all prescribed transaction costs. This equals existing cash on an
equity-only reset, but allows an annual reset to raise cash. If insufficient,
halt rather than silently substitute partial fills or borrow.

Use the existing total-return execution convention and tax lot reconstruction;
do not treat synthetic total-return units as real taxable shares. Base costs
are **10 bps of traded notional per side**, with fractional quantities.
No dollar minimum order is assumed in the research simulation.

The investment budget is fixed **at annual resets**, not every day. Asset
weights drift between resets; the equity bucket can become larger or smaller
than 30%. This is neither constant realized exposure nor constant risk.
The band limits trading opportunities; it does not guarantee low turnover or
tax efficiency. Both must be measured.

## 4. Comparators and one bounded experiment

Use three account policies, each evaluated at both **10 bps** and **20 bps**:

1. **Candidate:** the rule above. The 20-bps row changes costs only.
2. **Primary benchmark:** BA-002's annual equal-weight 60%-invested benchmark,
   unchanged, with costs matched to the candidate.
3. **No-signal control:** the same annual reset and quarterly equity-only
   opportunities/band as BA-003, but with constant within-equity targets of
   25% each. It uses its own holdings to decide band breaches. This matches the
   rebalancing policy, not the candidate's realized trade dates or turnover.

The control asks whether selecting leaders adds value beyond changing the
rebalancing policy. It cannot prove a risk-adjusted alpha: the tilt can still
change regional, currency and small-cap exposure. Report those allocations.

**No search grid:** no alternate lookbacks, tilt sizes, bands, review frequencies
or winner counts in this first experiment. The two cost rows are not two
candidates from which to select a winner.

Reuse both bounded, archived sources from the
[source-sensitivity protocol](../decisions/2026-09-05-ba002-source-sensitivity.md):
corrected Yahoo and normalized Tiingo, with the same DGS3MO cash input and archived exchange
calendar. Pin their existing manifest identities before any BA-003 run; do
not download replacements or choose the more favorable source.

- **Seen A:** 2007-06-01 through 2017-12-31.
- **Seen B:** 2018-01-01 through 2021-12-31.
- Keep the existing common data start, 2006-02-28, and evaluation starts for
  comparability, even though this signal needs less warmup than BA-002.
- Start each account/window at **$100,000 cash**, without inherited positions
  or tax carryovers. Use the same eight stylized tax scenarios and deduction-OFF
  scoring as the BA-002 diagnostic; pin its policy hash unchanged.

Both windows have already influenced the research. Neither is independent
validation for BA-003. Require complete prescribed windows; report each source
and window separately, not just a favorable pooled result. All observations
from **2022-01-01 onward remain outside this plan**. A BA-003 ID does not reset
the `BA-TREND` family holdout or authorize its use.

## 5. What would justify continuing?

For each source/window/cost row, define the conservative after-tax margin as
the candidate's **lowest** post-liquidation CAGR across the eight scenarios
minus the comparator's **highest** CAGR across those same scenarios.
Also report matched-scenario differences so the conservative envelope is clear.

The following proposed screen must pass in **both windows on both feeds**:

| Requirement | Base: 10 bps | Stress: 20 bps |
|---|---:|---:|
| Conservative margin over annual passive benchmark | At least +50 bps/year | Greater than zero |
| Conservative margin over no-signal control | Greater than zero | Greater than zero |
| Candidate maximum drawdown | At most 20%, and no worse than cost-matched annual benchmark | Same |

The 50-bps hurdle and drawdown ceilings preserve the user's earlier economic
requirements; the control comparison is a proposed additional attribution
check. Drawdown is daily peak-to-trough **pre-tax, after trading costs**, as in
BA-002, not an intraday loss limit or a promise about live losses. All identity,
calendar, finite-value and independent account-replay checks must pass before
scoring. A data/accounting failure means invalid evidence, not a strategy pass.

Keep the bar honest: the annual benchmark already showed approximately 20.5%
drawdown in seen A. Maintaining its investment budget does **not** automatically
satisfy the 20% ceiling. Do not loosen the ceiling if the tilt fails it.

The return hurdle is also demanding for a small tilt. In a simplified static,
pre-cost calculation, a 5%-of-account transfer needs a 10-percentage-point
annual return spread between leaders and laggards to add 0.5 percentage points
to portfolio return. This is arithmetic, not a forecast; compounding, drift,
costs and taxes change the realized result. At $2,000–$5,000, a 0.5% one-year
increment is only $10–$25 before unmodeled practical costs.

**Stopping rule:** complete and report the full prescribed screen even if an
early case fails an economic requirement. Any such failure then means no-go
for this candidate and ends this research exercise; do not use the holdout,
tune until it passes, or promote a favorable subset. Data/accounting failures
may halt immediately. A reproducible implementation defect may be corrected
with the original attempt retained, not hidden. Passing means only “worth
discussing a separately authorized forward paper test,” not “proven” or “ready
for funding.”

## 6. Implementation sequence after agreement

1. Agree on this draft's defaults, then record the exact rule, comparator,
   source, tax and period identities before historical computation. Reuse the
   existing evidence workflow; do not build another authorization system.
2. Add synthetic, discriminating tests first: exact 12–1 anchors; skipped-month
   changes cannot alter ranking; deterministic ties/all-negative scores;
   quarter-only scheduling; band values just below/at/above 0.05; no duplicate
   January trade; next-open gaps; and no trades outside the equity bucket on
   a quarterly reset. Tests must fail when the corresponding rule is broken.
3. Add the relative-rank policy and a narrow portfolio-aware rebalance mode.
   The current policy receives data but not holdings, and the planner targets
   the whole account: holding-aware bands and equity-only sizing need an
   explicit interface extension. The current planner treats omitted symbols
   as zero targets; passing only the four equity targets would liquidate the
   other ETFs. Make rebalance scope explicit and test this failure directly.
   Test cash/cost conservation, insufficient-cash rejection before the existing
   executor can scale buys, scale proportionality, and unchanged BA-001/002
   behavior; reconcile new fills through the existing tax replay.
4. Run the approved, fixed seen-history screen once. Preserve all six accounts
   per source/window, all tax scenarios, signals, band decisions, orders,
   fills, curves, input snapshots and identities. Report costs, turnover,
   realized tax drag, allocation drift and both comparator margins together.
5. Write one result note and stop at its verdict. Only if justified, separately
   design a prospective paper protocol and check $2,000–$5,000 order sizes,
   fractional-share availability and real operating costs before any funding.

## 7. Decisions proposed for approval

The hypothesis is agreed in principle; these exact defaults are **not yet
approved**: 12–1 ranking; top-two 10% / bottom-two 5% at annual reset; quarterly
reviews with the five-point within-equity band; and requiring positive excess
over the no-signal control. The proposed initial scope is deliberately one
rule plus one cost stress, with no new data purchase or holdout evaluation.
